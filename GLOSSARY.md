# Commander Template

A personal, non-competitive Commander deck-building template, shared with friends as a page that shows each category, its usual numbers, and its caveats.

## Language

**Commander (format)**:
The 100-card singleton format, usually played by four players.
_Avoid_: EDH (unless quoting)

**Commander (card)**:
The one card that leads a deck and sits in the command zone; written "your commander" in the template.
_Avoid_: General

**Template**:
The author's set of pillars, numbers and caveats for building a deck; the source file is `commander-template.md`.
_Avoid_: Guide, recipe

**Pillar**:
One of the three problems a deck must solve to function: Ramp, Card advantage, Removal. Each has a rule-of-thumb count that is a guideline, not a hard rule.
_Avoid_: Category (a category is anything in the template, including pillars)

**Ramp**:
Anything that gives more mana than lands alone, putting you ahead of one land per turn. Extra lands are not ramp.
_Avoid_: Mana (too broad)

**"Not a ramp" card**:
A mana-related card the author deliberately keeps out of the ramp *kinds* (rocks, dorks, spells) while still counting it toward the Ramp pillar. The name is a running joke.

**Land drop**:
The one land a player may play each turn; used to check whether a deck has enough lands.

**Guideline**:
A number or rule of thumb in the template that is advice, not a requirement; it is expected to shift from deck to deck, usually because of the commander or the colors.
_Avoid_: Rule, requirement, target
